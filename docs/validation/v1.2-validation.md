## 业务边界 / Business boundaries

- 仅合成研究输入；默认九项限额均为 null，不能自动推断用户可承受风险。Synthetic only; nine default limits remain unset.
- 正式考核仍未接入，当前仅为绝对偏差 × 合成费率。Formal assessments are unimplemented; the adapter uses an illustrative absolute-deviation fee.
- 支出代理量未覆盖保证金、授信和回款时序。Gross outflow is not margin, credit or cash-timing analysis.
- 压力场景无概率，不是 CVaR 或穷尽所有极端。Stress scenarios have no probabilities and do not exhaust extremes.
- 月度以月前单一时点覆盖整月，但沿用合成 DA/RT 账式；没有正式月度竞价/合同分解/考核适配。Monthly research is not a certified monthly market engine.
- 每月一次为使用与实验去重约束；未接入自动调度或唯一月度记录数据库。Monthly cadence is a workflow convention and experiment deduplication rule, not an automated scheduler.
- 事后差额不是可达收益、全局最优或 EVPI；后续实验登记不表示已验证。Hindsight gaps are not attainable gains, global optima or EVPI; experiments remain proposed.
- 未连接实盘交易、外部 LLM 或真实客户资料。No live exchange, hosted LLM or real-customer data integration.
